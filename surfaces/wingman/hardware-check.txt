Found, for a ruling (not changed): the Surface can put two packets in one notification when two buttons go down
(or up) at the same instant. The adapter drops such a notification whole, as Production Engineering's known-good
monitor and the prototype do, so neither press counts. Seen only for two buttons pressed together; a lone press
that shares a notification with another packet (another button's release, a T-bar move) would be lost the same
way, which this run did not show. The choice: keep it, or split a notification whose length is a multiple of 8
into packets, each checked on its own and handled in order (two buttons pressed together would then send the
first, and the in-flight rule would drop the second).
